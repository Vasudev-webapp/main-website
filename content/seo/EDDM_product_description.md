### (Ethylenedioxy)dimethanol (EDDM) — CAS 3586-55-8 | Industrial Biocide & H₂S Scavenger

**(Ethylenedioxy)dimethanol**, commonly known as **EDDM** (CAS Number: 3586-55-8, EINECS: 222-720-6), is a high-performance, slow-release formaldehyde-donating biocide and hydrogen sulfide scavenger. With the molecular formula C₄H₁₀O₄ and a molecular weight of 122.12 g/mol, EDDM is a colorless to light yellow transparent liquid that is fully miscible with water, making it ideal for a wide range of water-based industrial applications.

#### How EDDM Works — Mechanism of Action

EDDM functions as a **controlled-release formaldehyde donor**. Upon contact with water-based systems, it slowly releases formaldehyde at concentrations sufficient to inhibit microbial growth without the hazards associated with direct formaldehyde handling. This slow-release mechanism provides sustained antimicrobial protection and effective **headspace (gas-phase) sterilization** within sealed containers.

As an **H₂S scavenger**, EDDM reacts selectively with hydrogen sulfide at room temperature in an exothermic reaction, converting toxic H₂S into non-hazardous byproducts. Unlike triazine-based scavengers, EDDM operates at neutral to slightly acidic pH, does not produce salt precipitates that clog pipelines, does not affect downstream catalyst activity, and maintains effectiveness across a broad pH range (4–11).
